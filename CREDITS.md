# Credits

T-MINUS web edition is a re-implementation of the Unity project in `../Original_Project` (credits there apply in full). Game logic, data tables, text, the Space Guide, the Blender part models and the PBR texture art come from that project.

## NASA and public science resources

- **Lake Manchar / Landsat flood images:** NASA Earth Observatory, “Lake Manchar Is Overflowing.” Credit line: **“NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Landsat data from the U.S. Geological Survey.”** Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/lake-manchar-is-overflowing-150306/ . Bundled at 1024 px in `public/images/`.
- **Earth texture:** NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation, topography and bathymetry, August 2004, by Reto Stöckli, **"NASA Earth Observatory."** https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/ — `public/images/BlueMarble.jpg` (4096 × 2048).
- **Optional live pictures:** NASA Worldview Snapshots (MODIS Terra true colour), fetched on demand for non-bundled target regions.
- **Landsat program:** NASA and the U.S. Geological Survey. https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions
- **Saturn V facts:** from the Wikipedia Saturn V article, compared on 14 November 2026 (see the original CREDITS). A procedural model is used.
- **Rehbar-I historical context:** Pakistan's first sounding rocket launched from Sonmiani in 1962 with NASA support. Verify against a mission-specific archival source before public submission.
